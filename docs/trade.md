# Markets and trade

Select a **market station** on comms (your Haven, the Yards, and on some maps your
spire) and fly within about **900** of it.

## Selling

**Sell the hold** turns everything you carry into credits, and it **refits your lift
rings** too.

| Cargo | From | Credits per unit |
|---|---|---|
| Coolant | ice clouds | 1 |
| Fuel | sulfur clouds | 2 |
| Materials | ore dust | 3 |
| Medicine | spore drifts | 3 |
| Salvage | wreck haze | 5 |
| Aether | aether veins | 8 |

A full cruiser hold (250 units) of ore sells for about 750.

## Trading cargo for refits

**Trade cargo for refits** spends cargo instead of selling it. Each refit appears only when
you are carrying enough to pay for it.

| Refit | Costs | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Restock missiles | 40 fuel | refills every missile type |
| Refit lift rings | 20 aether | lift back to full and shields charged |
| Enlarge the hold | 60 materials + 20 salvage | +100 hold, for the rest of the game |
| Bolt on plating | 50 materials | shields to full |

## Selling or trading?

Credits win the Salvage map. Refits keep you alive long enough to earn them. Enlarging the
hold early pays for itself over a long game, and a missile restock before a big raid can
matter more than a single sale.

**Docking** at a market station refits your rings for free. It's slower than selling,
but it costs no cargo.
