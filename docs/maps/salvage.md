# Salvage Above Venus

*Harvest the resource clouds, strip the wrecks, and haul it all home before time runs out.
Raiders hunt the lanes, storms tear at your rings, and a ship that loses its lift sinks.*

![Riding the murk home with a full hold](../img/chase_amber.jpg)

## The job

Your faction's colony needs money. You start beside your home **Haven**. Somewhere out
in 30 km of cloud sea are resource clouds and old wrecks. Fill your hold, fly home, and
sell. Reach the **credit target** before the clock runs out.

| | |
|---|---|
| Target | 800 + 200 per difficulty level for one crew. Each extra crew adds 70%. At difficulty 4, one crew needs **1600 credits**. |
| Time | Game Length, 25 minutes by default |
| You win | when the credits reach the target |
| You lose | when time runs out, your Haven is destroyed, or every ship is lost |

## Where to sell

There are two market stations: your **Haven** at the center, and a second **Yards**
station run by a faction at peace with yours. Select either on comms and fly within
about 900 of it to sell. See [Markets and trade](../trade.md) for prices, and for trading
cargo for refits instead.

## What comes for you

- **Raids** arrive every few minutes. At difficulty 4 that is every 3 minutes, and faster
  at higher difficulty. They are fleets from the factions at war with yours, and they
  arrive **5-7 km from one of your ships**, so they will find you. The fleets grow every
  other wave.
- **Storm cells** (dark clouds with lightning) strike ships inside them every few seconds
  and damage your systems.
- **The deck.** Every hit can knock out a lift ring, and a ship that cannot hold its
  altitude sinks.

## Tips

- **Your hold is limited.** A cruiser carries 250 units, so every trip ends with a flight
  home. Plan which clouds to hit.
- **Aether and salvage pay best** (8 and 5 credits a unit). Ice pays least (1).
- **Harvesting wrecks repairs your rings** as you salvage them, a little at a time.
- **Wind lanes** run out from the Haven and back in again. Fly with the wind for 1.6x
  speed.
- **Hide, don't fight, when you are low.** A cloud hides you from missiles and turrets.
- **Selling refits your rings.** A trip home is also a repair.
