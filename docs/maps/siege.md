# Siege of the Haven

*Every hostile navy in the sky has come for the Haven. Hold the command spire until the
storm season breaks.*

## The job

Your faction's **command spire** stands at the center, with its industrial yards and civil
Haven beside it. All three are armed with turrets, and all three are market stations
where you can sell and refit. Survive until the clock runs out.

| | |
|---|---|
| Time | Game Length, 15 minutes by default |
| You win | when the spire is still standing at the end |
| You lose | when the command spire is destroyed, or every defender is lost |

## The waves

Waves come every one to three minutes, faster at higher difficulty. Each is a fleet from a
faction at war with yours, it grows every time, and it arrives **11-14 km out, from any
direction**. These fleets go for **stations first**.

## Tips

- **Meet them out in the clouds.** A wave that reaches the spire is fighting its turrets
  and you at once, but it is also firing on the spire.
- **Dock between waves.** Docking at any of your three stations refits your lift rings
  for free.
- **Resource clouds are close by.** Harvest between waves and trade the cargo for refits:
  a missile restock, ring repairs, or shields.
- **Your faction's ability decides how you defend.** Flak Curtain (Syndicate) clears
  incoming missiles; Regrowth (Brood) keeps you in the fight longer. See
  [the six factions](../factions.md).
