# Fleet Battle Above Venus

*Two navies, one sky. Break the enemy's command spire before they break yours. Your own
fleet flies with you, and theirs keeps coming.*

![A storm cell over the lanes](../img/chase_storm.jpg)

## The job

You choose your **Faction** and the **Enemy** in the settings panel. Your **command
spire** stands at the center, with your yards beside it. The enemy's spire and yards are
**26 km away** across the cloud sea. Wind lanes run both ways between them.

| | |
|---|---|
| You win | when the enemy command spire is destroyed |
| You lose | when your spire falls, or every player ship is lost |

## The battle

- **Your fleet** starts beside you. It grows with difficulty and takes the fight to the
  enemy on its own.
- **The enemy fleet** waits near their spire, and it is at least as big as yours.
- **Reinforcements.** The enemy sends a new squadron every few minutes (about every 3
  minutes at difficulty 4). Your side sends one every other wave, so the longer the
  battle goes on, the worse the odds get.
- **Stations fight back.** Command spires carry turrets. Strip them before you close in.

## Tips

- **Ride the wind lane toward the enemy.** You arrive faster, and your fleet follows the
  same line.
- **Your ships fire missiles**, and so do theirs. Turrets shoot down missiles, so a
  target covered by several turrets needs several volleys.
- **Fall back to a cloud** when you are hurt, or to your own yards to repair.
- **Watch the deck.** A crippled ship sinks, and chasing the enemy downward is how you
  end up too low to come back.
