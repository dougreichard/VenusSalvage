# Convoy to the Yards

*Three barges and a tender, 30 km of open sky to the Yards, and raiders who know the
route. Bring at least two of them home.*

## The job

Your faction's **Haven** is at the start and your **Yards** are 30 km away. A convoy of
**three cargo barges and a steam tender** sets out along the wind lane between them. It
flies on its own; your job is to keep it alive.

| | |
|---|---|
| You win | when **two** convoy ships reach the Yards |
| You lose | when too few convoy ships are left to make two, or every escort is lost |

## The threat

Raiders strike every couple of minutes, sooner at higher difficulty, and each wave is
bigger than the last. They appear **5-6.5 km from the lead barge**, and they go for the
**convoy first**. Escorts come second.

## Tips

- **Fly out ahead.** Raids arrive near the lead barge, so an escort already out in front
  meets them before they reach the convoy.
- **Barges carry no weapons.** Every raider that reaches them is a barge lost.
- **Two is enough.** Once two ships are safe the job is done, so you can pull back and
  guard the last stretch.
- **Use cloud cover to break contact** when you are hurt, then rejoin.
