# The six factions

Every faction salvaged a different people's starships, so every fleet looks different.
All of them fly the same kinds of ship on the same lift rings.

| Faction | Built from | Their smoke | Ability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salvage Flotilla** | wrecked starship hulls, rebuilt as riverboats with paddlewheels and stacks | white steam | **Boiler Vent** |
| **Aurelian Concord** | chrome ocean-liner starships hung under deco zeppelin envelopes | white steam | **Gas Trim** |
| **Atomic Directorate** | finned rocket ships; a reactor glows inside every lift ring | green motes | **Reactor Overcharge** |
| **Iron Syndicate** | armored warships with rivets, propellers and hazard stripes | black exhaust | **Flak Curtain** |
| **Sunward Commons** | light eco-ships with gardens, trees and solar-sail wings | pollen | **Sail Sprint** |
| **Verdant Brood** | living bio-ships regrown into flying creatures | spores | **Regrowth** |

## Abilities

Use your ability from the **Airship** app on the ePADD. It takes a while to recharge.

| Ability | What it does | Lasts | Recharge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boiler Vent | A steam screen hides your ship as if it were in a cloud | 12 s | 75 s |
| Gas Trim | Restores about a third of your lift at once | instant | 90 s |
| Reactor Overcharge | Shields to full, and half again your speed | 12 s | 90 s |
| Flak Curtain | Shoots down every missile within 1500 | instant | 60 s |
| Sail Sprint | Double speed | 10 s | 60 s |
| Regrowth | Lift and shields slowly grow back | 20 s | 120 s |

## Who is at war with whom

| | At war with |
|---|---|
| Salvage Flotilla | the Brood. They trade with everyone else. |
| Aurelian Concord | the Syndicate and the Brood |
| Atomic Directorate | the Syndicate and the Brood |
| Iron Syndicate | the Concord, the Directorate, the Commons and the Brood. They take what others build. |
| Sunward Commons | the Syndicate and the Brood |
| Verdant Brood | **everyone**. The Brood is a hunger, not a nation. |

Raids in [Salvage](maps/salvage.md), [Convoy](maps/convoy.md) and
[Siege](maps/siege.md) come from the factions at war with yours. Fly the **Flotilla** for
the fewest enemies. Fly the **Brood** and everyone is coming for you, and the only
friendly markets are your own.
